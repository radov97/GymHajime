# FindPalco
🌍 FindPalco – Bridging Travelers & Locals Through Shared Experiences
🧭 Introduction
FindPalco is a global community-driven platform that connects travelers and locals through real-world experiences — performances, classes, gatherings, and artistic expressions. The name Palco, meaning “stage” in several languages, reflects the heart of our vision: every community is a stage, and every person has something to share.

Whether you’re backpacking through Vietnam or living in a small town in the Philippines, FindPalco offers you a way to connect, collaborate, perform, host, or simply join an event happening in your area — created not by companies, but by real people.

🔑 Key Concepts
Travelers meet locals: The platform enables cultural exchange by letting visitors participate in community events, classes, or performances hosted by locals.

Locals earn & express: From artists and teachers to hosts and performers, locals can share their skills, rent their space, or host experiences for others to join.

Not just places — stories: FindPalco is not an Airbnb for venues. It’s a bridge to people, traditions, creativity, and personal connection.

Accessible to all: Designed to be mobile-friendly and lightweight — usable in cities or rural villages, even with modest internet.

🎯 Core Features
Discover Experiences: Browse available events or sessions based on your location, time, interest (e.g., music, dance, wellness, crafts).

Host or Perform: Locals can post opportunities — from offering a backyard for acoustic nights to teaching salsa in a town square.

Book & Join: Travelers can easily request to join, pay securely, and even leave reviews.

Multilingual & Inclusive: Built to support cross-cultural connection and local languages.

💡 Real-Life Use Cases
Gem, a teacher in Mobo, Philippines, wants to organize a dance showcase. She lists the event on FindPalco. A Spanish tourist sees it, books a ticket, and even joins the warm-up class — making friends along the way.

Alex, a Brazilian guitarist traveling through Portugal, finds a listing for an open mic hosted in a local’s living room in Porto. He performs, connects with other musicians, and is invited to another jam session the next day.

Fatima, a young Moroccan artist, offers watercolor painting lessons in her courtyard. An American digital nomad books it via FindPalco and ends up bonding over mint tea while learning traditional patterns.

🎯 Overall Goal
To foster meaningful human connections through local spaces, talents, and stories.
FindPalco aims to become the go-to platform for cultural discovery, grassroots expression, and people-powered performance — wherever you go.

💰 How FindPalco Makes Money
FindPalco uses a simple and transparent monetization model focused on service value, not user exploitation. Our mission is to empower locals while sustaining the platform. Here's how revenue is generated:

🔄 1. Commission on Bookings
We charge a small service fee on every confirmed booking made through the platform.

Example: A traveler books a ₱1,000 dance class. FindPalco takes a 10–15% commission (e.g., ₱100–150), while the rest goes directly to the host.

This fee supports secure payment processing, customer support, and ongoing development.

🧑‍🎨 2. Featured Listings (Optional)
Local hosts can optionally boost their listings for better visibility in search results (like promoted posts).

Ideal for teachers, performers, or venue owners who want to attract more participants.

🧾 3. Subscription for Pro Hosts (Future Feature)
Power users — such as event spaces or regular hosts — can opt for a Pro Host Plan to access premium features:

Analytics and booking insights

Auto-replies and availability sync

Branded profile pages

Lower transaction fees
